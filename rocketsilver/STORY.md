## Part One: Johto Campaign

### Mission 1: Game Opening — The New Bark Heist

The game begins at Team Rocket’s Mahogany base. Silver stands beside Karen and Will, who serve in roles similar to Andra’s in *Pokémon Team Rocket Edition*. In Professor Oak’s customary introductory role, the Iron-Masked Marauder sets the stage by recounting Team Rocket’s fall and Giovanni’s disappearance.

Silver is introduced to Team Rocket’s four executives: Archer, Ariana, Proton, and Petrel. The Iron-Masked Marauder explains that Silver, Will, and Karen were selected as special agents because Team Rocket recognized their potential at a young age.

Each agent receives a partner Pokémon:

- Silver: Shiny Larvitar
- Will: Natu
- Karen: Sneasel

Will’s Natu transports the three agents to Cherrygrove City for their first assignment. Their objectives are divided as follows:

- Will must steal evolution research from Professor Oak’s temporary Johto residence in Cherrygrove.
- Silver must infiltrate Professor Elm’s laboratory in New Bark Town and steal a starter Pokémon.
- Karen must investigate Mr. Pokémon’s house and identify the mysterious Pokémon Egg in his possession: Togepi.

After stealing a starter from Professor Elm, Silver encounters Gold on the route back to Cherrygrove and battles him for the first time. Silver then reunites with Will and Karen. Once all three objectives have been completed, Natu transports the group back to Mahogany base to report their success.

### Mission 2: Sprout Tower Intelligence

Team Rocket believes Sprout Tower contains historical records concerning Lugia, Ho-Oh, the destruction of the Brass Tower, and the legendary beasts Suicune, Raikou, and Entei.

Will accompanies Silver on the mission. They climb Sprout Tower and battle its trainers as they search for the records. At the top of the tower, Silver and Will fight Falkner and Zuki of the Kimono Girls in a double battle.

After defeating them, the agents seize the records and return them to Team Rocket.

### Mission 3: Ruins of Alph — First Solo Mission

Silver receives his first solo assignment: investigate the Ruins of Alph and capture an Unown for Team Rocket’s researchers. Team Rocket believes the Unown possess power connected to creation and could be useful in several future projects.

Silver may fly to Violet City using one of his own Pokémon. If he does not have a Pokémon capable of flying, Archer loans him a Skarmory for transportation.

Inside the ruins, Silver uses Rock Smash with his Shiny Larvitar or its current evolved form to open a sealed chamber. Within it, he discovers inscriptions concerning Celebi, described as a forest guardian capable of opening a doorway through time.

Silver must capture an Unown and defeat the fossil Pokémon guarding the chamber: Aerodactyl, Kabutops, and Omastar.

As Silver leaves the Ruins of Alph, Gold confronts him and attempts to recover what Team Rocket has stolen. Silver defeats Gold and escapes with the Unown and the inscriptions.

### Mission 4: Slowpoke Well Diversion

Proton leads a Team Rocket operation at Slowpoke Well, assisted by Will and Karen. The public purpose of the operation is to harvest Slowpoke tails and raise money for Team Rocket. Its more important purpose is to create a diversion that draws Kurt and Gold away from Kurt’s house.

During the operation, Will encounters and catches a Slowpoke whose psychic potential interests him. It remains part of his team and eventually evolves into Slowking as Will grows stronger alongside Silver.

While the others occupy Slowpoke Well, Silver infiltrates Kurt’s home. His objective is to steal Kurt’s prototype Poké Ball designs and an inscription describing a special ball created through the combination of two sacred feathers: the Silver Wing and the Rainbow Wing. These materials will eventually contribute to the creation of the GS Ball, although Silver is not told this at the time.

After Silver secures the prototypes and inscription, Naoko of the Kimono Girls arrives with two police officers. Naoko has been watching Silver’s movements and attempts to prevent his escape.

Silver battles the two officers and Naoko, then flees into Ilex Forest. Bugsy intercepts him before he can reach the shrine and challenges him to another battle.

After defeating Bugsy, Silver reaches the shrine clearing and encounters the Iron-Masked Marauder. The Marauder speaks cryptically about the shrine and the legends claiming it is connected to the Voice of the Forest Pokémon.

Silver then returns to Mahogany base with the stolen materials.

### Mission 5: Goldenrod Radio Tower Reconnaissance

Silver is sent to infiltrate Goldenrod Radio Tower and study its broadcasting equipment. Team Rocket’s scientists want to understand how the tower generates, amplifies, and directs radio signals so the technology can be adapted for future Pokémon experiments.

To gain access to the building’s restricted upper floors, Silver first completes the Radio Quiz and obtains legitimate clearance. As he climbs the tower, he battles employees and security personnel who question why he is entering restricted areas.

Once Silver reaches the broadcast archives, he searches for technical records concerning the tower’s equipment. He obtains two important sets of information:

- Schematics explaining how Goldenrod Radio Tower amplifies a signal across an entire region.
- Equipment records showing how specific frequencies can be isolated, redirected, and sustained without interrupting ordinary broadcasts.

As Silver attempts to leave the tower, Whitney confronts him. She explains that people have become suspicious of his activities and demands to know why he was searching the restricted floors.

Silver defeats Whitney and escapes into Goldenrod Tunnel, using its underground passages to avoid the authorities gathering around the Radio Tower. Kuni of the Kimono Girls intercepts him in the tunnel. Having already become suspicious of Team Rocket’s interest in Johto’s legends and communications network, she attempts to seize the stolen broadcast information.

Silver defeats Kuni and escapes Goldenrod with the technical records. He returns to Mahogany base, where the mission is declared a complete success. Team Rocket’s scientists begin adapting what he learned into portable transmitters for future experiments.

### Mission 6: The Ecruteak Earthquake

Silver and Karen are assigned to investigate the Burned Tower and Bell Tower for evidence that Ho-Oh has recently returned to Ecruteak City.

The Iron-Masked Marauder also loans Silver his Piloswine. Silver is ordered to use it beneath the Ecruteak Dance Theater—the approximate center of the city—to create a controlled tremor. Team Rocket hopes that Ho-Oh will appear if it senses that Ecruteak is in danger.

Silver and Karen begin at the Burned Tower, where they overhear Morty and Eusine discussing the local legends. The legendary beasts are said to rest beneath the tower, Ho-Oh is believed to guard Ecruteak from afar, and Lugia is said to dwell in the Whirl Islands.

Morty and Eusine notice the Rocket agents and confront them for trespassing in a sacred place. Silver and Karen defeat them and proceed to the Dance Theater.

Inside the theater, they battle Miki of the Kimono Girls. After defeating her, Silver uses Piloswine to trigger the tremor. The earthquake causes limited damage throughout the city, but Ho-Oh does not appear.

Silver and Karen return to Mahogany base and report everything they learned, as well as the failure to draw out Ho-Oh, to the Iron-Masked Marauder.

### Mission 7: Olivine Lighthouse — Signal Components

Team Rocket’s scientists have completed their study of the records Silver stole from Goldenrod Radio Tower, but they still lack the specialized components needed to build a powerful portable transmitter. Petrel identifies the Glitter Lighthouse in Olivine City as the best source for a high-output focusing lens and signal converter.

Silver is sent to enter the lighthouse, reach its upper equipment room, and steal the required components. He battles the lighthouse’s trainers and security personnel while climbing toward the beacon.

At the summit, Jasmine realizes that Silver has not come to help Olivine or its Ampharos. She refuses to let Team Rocket dismantle the lighthouse and challenges him. Silver defeats Jasmine while Rocket technicians remove a spare focusing lens and converter from the maintenance system.

The theft temporarily weakens the lighthouse beam but leaves Ampharos and the primary beacon intact. Silver returns to Mahogany with the components, allowing Team Rocket’s scientists to begin constructing the portable transmitter later used in the Lake of Rage and Whirl Islands experiments.

### Mission 8: Secure the Safari Zone and Cliff Cave

Ariana and Petrel lead a large Team Rocket operation to seize Cliff Cave and the Johto Safari Zone. Silver, Will, Karen, and ten Rocket grunts participate.

The operation has three objectives:

- Secure the rare Pokémon within the Safari Zone.
- Establish control over Cliff Cave and the surrounding routes.
- Use the area’s proximity to the Whirl Islands to begin searching for Lugia.

Because the Safari Zone houses many rare Pokémon, it is defended by a large security force. During the advance, the Rocket unit battles security guards and four visiting trainers from Sinnoh: Mira, Buck, Cheryl, and Marley.

At the Safari Zone gates, Riley and Chuck confront the attackers. Silver and Ariana face them together in a double battle.

After entering the Safari Zone, Silver must defeat four additional security guards to secure the facility and complete the takeover.

Silver returns to Mahogany base with Petrel, Will, and Karen. Ariana remains behind with the Rocket grunts to fortify the Safari Zone, capture its rare Pokémon, and begin the search for Lugia in the Whirl Islands.

### Mission 9: The Dragon Fang and the Red Gyarados Experiment

Silver is sent alone to Blackthorn City with orders to infiltrate Dragon’s Den and retrieve the Dragon Fang. Team Rocket’s scientists believe the artifact contains the concentrated draconic energy needed to complete the first prototype Rocket Stone. If successful, the stone will allow Team Rocket to force controlled mutations in exceptionally powerful Pokémon.

Silver enters Dragon’s Den and fights his way past members of the Dragon Clan. As he approaches the inner shrine, Sayo of the Kimono Girls confronts him. Aware of Team Rocket’s growing interest in Johto’s sacred relics, she attempts to prevent Silver from reaching the Dragon Fang.

Silver defeats Sayo and continues into the shrine. Deep within the den, Clair confronts him and refuses to surrender the Fang. Silver defeats Clair, takes the Dragon Fang, and escapes Blackthorn before the clan can seal the den.

Upon returning to Mahogany, Silver delivers the Fang to Team Rocket’s scientists in the Deep Rocket Base. The Fang is installed as the prototype Rocket Stone’s power core, providing the means to channel draconic energy into a living Pokémon.

The scientists immediately begin the experiment at the Lake of Rage. Team Rocket’s evolution signal forces the Magikarp in the lake to evolve, while the Rocket Stone focuses the Dragon Fang’s energy upon one exceptional Gyarados. The experiment transforms it into the Red Gyarados, an artificially altered Water/Dragon Pokémon and Team Rocket’s first successful Rocket mutation.

The experiment leaves the Red Gyarados enraged and uncontrollable. Silver is ordered to battle and capture it before it can escape the lake. After defeating the Red Gyarados, Silver catches it and claims it as his own Pokémon.

Meanwhile, Clair reports the Dragon Fang’s theft to Lance. Lance follows Silver’s trail from Blackthorn to Mahogany with Gold and realizes that Team Rocket is operating from the old surface hideout. Shortly after Silver captures the Red Gyarados, Lance and Gold launch an assault on the facility.

Team Rocket initiates an emergency lockdown. The concealed entrance to the Deep Rocket Base is sealed before the attackers penetrate the upper facility. Lance defeats Karen and Will, while Gold and Lance destroy much of Team Rocket’s visible operation. Neither of them discovers the hidden complex beneath the hideout.

Silver returns from the Lake of Rage after the upper base has been destroyed. He finds Karen and Will defeated and confronts Lance and Gold before they can withdraw. Archer joins Silver, beginning a double battle between Silver and Archer and the opposing team of Lance and Gold.

Silver and Archer win the battle. Lance refuses to consider the operation a failure, warning Silver that he will return with his full force next time. He withdraws with Gold, declaring that he has already accomplished his immediate objective by crippling Team Rocket’s visible operation.

The Deep Rocket Base remains operational and hidden. Silver already has access to this restricted headquarters, which contains:

- The executives’ command center.
- Team Rocket’s scientific laboratories.
- Research concerning the Rocket Stones and altered Pokémon.
- Intelligence files on rare and legendary Pokémon.
- Specialized training facilities and EV-training equipment.

Although the upper hideout has been devastated, the Deep Rocket Base remains intact. Beneath the ruins, Silver, Archer, the other executives, and Team Rocket’s scientists survive with the Dragon Fang, the prototype Rocket Stone, and the newly captured Red Gyarados.

### Mission 10: Shadow Lugia — The Silver Wing

Following Lance’s attack, Team Rocket is weakened but not destroyed. Ariana’s unit remains stationed near the Safari Zone and reports that its scouts have located the deepest chamber of the Whirl Islands—the same hidden cavern in which Lugia is said to sleep.

The Iron-Masked Marauder personally takes command of the operation. He explains that the Lake of Rage experiment proved Team Rocket’s radio technology can enrage and alter exceptionally powerful Pokémon. A portable transmitter will now be used to force Lugia out of the deep water, allowing Team Rocket to capture it and obtain the Silver Wing.

Silver travels to the Whirl Islands with the Iron-Masked Marauder and Ariana’s remaining forces. The Marauder directs the operation but remains behind the main advance with the transmitter controls. He has no intention of risking his own Pokémon against Lugia when Silver can do the fighting for him.

Silver leads the grunts through the islands’ violent currents and collapsed passages, defeating the wild Pokémon and guardians blocking the path. Once Silver secures the deepest chamber, Rocket technicians carry in the portable transmitter and position its emitters over the water.

Once the transmitter is installed in Lugia’s chamber, the Iron-Masked Marauder orders the signal activated. The radio waves penetrate the water and drive Lugia into a violent rage. Lugia erupts from the depths, but the sustained frequency corrupts its aura and transforms it into Shadow Lugia.

Shadow Lugia overwhelms the Rocket grunts and threatens to destroy the transmitter. The Marauder refuses to enter the battle and orders Silver to subdue it while he continues directing the radio signal from a protected position. After a major battle, Silver defeats and captures Shadow Lugia.

With Lugia secured, the Iron-Masked Marauder removes a single silver feather and claims it as the Silver Wing. Silver retains Shadow Lugia as his captured Pokémon, while the Wing is taken back to the Deep Rocket Base for the Marauder’s secret project.

The operation confirms that Team Rocket’s radio technology can draw out and alter a legendary Pokémon. With Shadow Lugia captured and the Silver Wing secured, the Iron-Masked Marauder immediately turns his attention to Ho-Oh and the sacred tower overlooking Ecruteak City.

### Mission 11: Assault on Tin Tower — The Rainbow Wing

The Iron-Masked Marauder leads Silver, Karen, and Will in a direct assault on Tin Tower. Their objective is to install the portable transmitter at the summit, use its radio waves to draw Ho-Oh down from the sky, capture it, and obtain the Rainbow Wing—the second sacred feather required for the Marauder’s hidden plan.

The five Kimono Girls have anticipated Team Rocket’s attack. Zuki, Naoko, Miki, Kuni, and Sayo take positions throughout Tin Tower as its final security force, bringing together all five opponents Silver has encountered during earlier missions.

At the tower entrance, Silver and Karen face Zuki and Naoko in a double battle. Karen’s stronger team demonstrates how far she has progressed since the beginning of the campaign, with her original Sneasel now evolved into Weavile and supported by Umbreon, Murkrow or Honchkrow, and her altered Dark/Ghost Gengar.

On the middle floors, Silver and Will battle Miki and Kuni together. Will now fights with his evolved Xatu and the Slowpoke he rescued from the well, which has become Slowking, alongside Hypno R and Bronzong.

Near the summit, Sayo makes the Kimono Girls’ final stand. Silver defeats her while Karen and Will hold off the remaining tower defenders, clearing the way for Rocket technicians to carry the transmitter onto the roof.

At the summit, the Iron-Masked Marauder orders the transmitter activated. Its signal uses the Goldenrod broadcast research and the focusing components stolen from Olivine Lighthouse to project a concentrated frequency across the sky. The radio waves disturb Ho-Oh and force it to descend toward Tin Tower.

Ho-Oh attacks the Rocket group at the summit and attempts to destroy the transmitter. The sustained signal reacts with Ho-Oh’s sacred aura in the opposite manner from Lugia’s corruption. Its body becomes brilliant white, its wings intensify to radiant gold and orange, and a violet luminous aura gathers around it. Ho-Oh is transformed into **Shining Ho-Oh**, the purified counterpart to Shadow Lugia.

As he did during the Lugia operation, the Marauder remains beside the controls and orders Silver to face the legendary Pokémon alone. He is content to let Silver accept every risk while he observes the battle and maintains the signal. Silver defeats and captures Shining Ho-Oh.

Once Shining Ho-Oh has been secured, the Iron-Masked Marauder extracts a single rainbow-colored feather and claims it as the Rainbow Wing. Silver retains Shining Ho-Oh, while the Marauder returns to the Deep Rocket Base with both the Rainbow Wing and the Silver Wing.

The five Kimono Girls survive the assault and realize that their earlier encounters with Silver were parts of a single campaign to seize Johto’s sacred Pokémon and artifacts. With both Wings now in his possession, the Iron-Masked Marauder turns his attention toward the Pokémon League tournament at Indigo Plateau, where the strongest trainers from Kanto and Johto will soon gather.

### Mission 12: The Indigo Plateau Tournament

The Pokémon League hosts an enormous tournament at Indigo Plateau, bringing together all eight Gym Leaders from Kanto and all eight Gym Leaders from Johto. The event is watched from the upper grandstand by Lorelei, Bruno, Agatha, and Lance, who presides over the tournament as its strongest defender.

Team Rocket uses the gathering as the centerpiece of a massive diversion. Archer, Ariana, Proton, and Petrel lead Rocket grunts into the stadium and launch a coordinated assault on the sixteen Gym Leaders. The attack traps nearly every major defender of Kanto and Johto inside one location and prevents them from interfering with the Iron-Masked Marauder’s true operation.

While the executives and grunts occupy the Gym Leaders, Silver, Karen, and Will force their way toward the upper grandstand. Their objective is to defeat the League’s strongest trainers and prevent Lance from breaking through the Rocket assault.

Will confronts Bruno, using Xatu, Slowking, Hypno R, and Bronzong against Bruno’s physically powerful team. Karen faces Agatha in a battle between two generations of Dark- and Ghost-type specialists. Silver battles Lorelei with his full team, now including Shadow Lugia and Shining Ho-Oh.

After Lorelei, Bruno, and Agatha are defeated, Silver reaches Lance. Karen and Will are too exhausted to continue, leaving Silver to face him alone.

Lance reveals that he held back during the siege of Mahogany. His full team is built around a unique Dragonite form that Silver has never encountered. This Dragonite is Lance’s greatest Pokémon and the true source of his reputation as the most powerful trainer in the region.

Silver’s possession of Shadow Lugia and Shining Ho-Oh gives him the power needed to withstand Lance’s Dragonite and the rest of his championship team. After the campaign’s most difficult battle, Silver defeats Lance.

With Lance and the Elite Three defeated, the Rocket executives overwhelm the remaining resistance inside the stadium. Team Rocket takes control of Indigo Plateau, and the gathered Gym Leaders are left unable to pursue the Iron-Masked Marauder.

### Interlude: The Masked Man’s True Operation

While the Indigo Plateau assault unfolds, the Iron-Masked Marauder travels alone to the Ilex Forest shrine. Using Kurt’s stolen prototype designs together with the Silver Wing and Rainbow Wing, he completes the GS Ball.

Gold is waiting at the shrine with Suicune. He has followed the pattern behind Team Rocket’s thefts and realizes that the Marauder intends to use the shrine to reach Celebi. Gold challenges him, but the Marauder defeats Gold with ease.

Before the Marauder can activate the GS Ball, Suicune places itself between him and the shrine. The legendary Pokémon refuses to let him reach Celebi and continues defending Gold.

### Mission 13: Ilex Forest — Battle for Celebi

After securing Indigo Plateau, Silver learns that the tournament attack was only a diversion. He leaves the stadium and races to Ilex Forest, arriving after Gold has already been defeated.

Suicune rises to defend Gold as Silver enters the shrine clearing. Empowered by the shrine and driven by its determination to protect Celebi, Suicune assumes an enhanced state. Although Gold has been injured, he continues directing Suicune from behind it.

Silver battles Gold and the enhanced Suicune. After Silver wins, Suicune falls back beside Gold, leaving the path to the shrine open.

The Iron-Masked Marauder places the completed GS Ball before the shrine and uses the two sacred Wings to call Celebi through time. When Celebi appears, the GS Ball captures it before it can escape.

The Marauder opens a crack in time and tells Silver that they will travel into the past, bring Giovanni back, and right Team Rocket’s defeat. Silver enters the time passage with him, believing that their final mission is to prevent the organization’s fall.

### Mission 14: The Stolen Son — Final Battle with the Masked Man

Instead of taking them to the moment of Team Rocket’s defeat, Celebi brings Silver and the Iron-Masked Marauder to Viridian Forest three years in the past. Giovanni is there with a much younger Silver.

The time distortion forces Silver to witness the buried truth of his childhood: the Iron-Masked Marauder attacked Giovanni’s party, seized Silver as a boy, and carried him away to be raised as a weapon for his own plans. Team Rocket did not discover Silver’s potential and recruit him—the Marauder stole him from his father.

Giovanni sees the older Silver within the distortion and recognizes him as the future version of his missing son. He calls Silver by name, leaving Silver confused until the memory of the abduction becomes clear.

The Iron-Masked Marauder is furious with Celebi. He explains that the GS Ball was meant to take them to Team Rocket’s fall so he could rewrite history, not expose Silver’s past.

Silver finally understands that Giovanni is his father and that the man who raised him deliberately kept them apart. He turns on the Iron-Masked Marauder and demands the truth.

The Marauder admits that he needed a gifted child he could shape into a completely loyal agent. He claims that everything Silver became—his strength, his victories, and his ability to capture legendary Pokémon—exists because of him. Silver rejects that claim and challenges him.

The final battle pits Silver against the Iron-Masked Marauder’s complete Ice-type team: Delibird, Mamoswine, Regice, Glaceon, and Abomasnow. During the confrontation, the damaged mask reveals that the Marauder is Pryce, the Gym Leader of Mahogany Town.

Silver defeats Pryce. Unwilling to surrender Celebi or accept defeat, Pryce tears open another unstable crack in time and enters it alone. The passage collapses behind him, leaving Pryce lost somewhere within time.

Celebi releases Silver from the distortion and returns him to the present. Silver emerges carrying the truth of his identity: he is Giovanni’s son, stolen and raised by Pryce to serve a plan that was never truly Team Rocket’s.

## Part Two: Kanto Campaign — Operation Indigo

The Kanto campaign begins immediately after Silver returns from the time rift. Pryce is gone, the GS Ball has been lost, and Team Rocket’s occupation of Indigo Plateau cannot be maintained. Without the Iron-Masked Marauder’s hidden direction or a clear path to Giovanni, the organization fractures.

Karen and Will accept positions in the reconstructed Elite Four. Lorelei and Agatha retire following the attack on Indigo Plateau, while Bruno remains in office and Koga is promoted from Fuchsia Gym. The new Elite Four therefore consists of Will, Koga, Bruno, and Karen. Lance retains authority over the League while it rebuilds, although his defeat by Silver has weakened confidence in the old order.

Archer, Ariana, Proton, and Petrel abandon the damaged Mahogany operation and scatter with separate groups of followers. Some Rockets want to restore Giovanni’s criminal empire, some simply want to survive, and others believe Silver has earned the right to lead them.

Silver knows from the time distortion that Giovanni is his father, but he met only the Giovanni of three years ago. Giovanni’s present location remains unknown. Silver enters Kanto with three connected goals:

- Find his father and learn why Giovanni never returned to Team Rocket.
- Complete the Kanto Gym Challenge and force the League to recognize him openly.
- Decide whether Team Rocket should disappear, return to its old methods, or become the force that unites Kanto and Johto as one Indigo region.

The League permits Silver to undertake the Gym Challenge under strict observation. Every Gym Leader may refuse his challenge until he resolves the crisis affecting that Leader’s city. The Badges therefore represent more than victories: they are proof that Silver can exercise power without relying on a Rocket occupation.

### Mission 15: The Broken R — Withdrawal from Indigo

Silver returns from the time rift to find the Indigo Plateau assault ending in disorder. Rocket grunts are retreating, the Gym Leaders are being released, and the four executives blame one another for following the Masked Man’s plan.

Archer attempts to assume command. He argues that Team Rocket has already defeated the League and should exploit the confusion by conquering Kanto before Lance can recover. Ariana refuses to sacrifice the surviving grunts in another immediate assault. Proton leaves with the most violent members of the organization, while Petrel disappears with copies of Rocket’s intelligence files.

Karen and Will announce that they will remain at Indigo Plateau. With Lorelei and Agatha retiring, Lance offers them places beside Bruno and the newly promoted Koga in the Elite Four. They accept so they can help prevent either the League or Team Rocket from controlling both regions without opposition.

Silver refuses to surrender Team Rocket to Archer, but he also refuses to disband it before finding Giovanni. Archer departs for Kanto with stolen Rocket Stone research and declares that Giovanni’s true successor will be decided there.

Lance strips Silver of any claim created by the stadium takeover. He offers Silver one lawful path to influence over Kanto: defeat all eight Kanto Gym Leaders and return to Indigo Plateau as a recognized challenger.

> **Lance:** “You conquered a stadium while its defenders were divided. That is not the same as earning the trust of two regions.”
>
> **Silver:** “I don’t need their trust.”
>
> **Karen:** “Then earn their recognition. Trust may come later.”

Silver crosses into Kanto alone. Shadow Lugia and Shining Ho-Oh remain available to him, but several major opponents develop teams specifically intended to withstand legendary Pokémon.

### Mission 16: Pewter City — The First Judgment

Silver reaches Pewter City and requests his first official Kanto Gym battle. Brock refuses. A band of former Rocket grunts has raided the Pewter Museum and stolen fossil specimens, causing the city to believe Silver’s arrival marks the beginning of another Rocket operation.

Silver tracks the thieves through the museum basement and into Mt. Moon. The grunts reveal that Proton hired them to steal fossil material for sale to scientists attempting to reproduce Team Rocket’s mutation experiments.

Silver defeats the thieves, recovers the fossils, and destroys their unstable revival equipment. Brock then agrees to battle him, explaining that the battle will test whether Silver can solve a crisis without turning every useful Pokémon or artifact into a weapon.

After Silver defeats Brock, he receives the Boulder Badge. Brock also gives him the first credible Kanto lead concerning Giovanni: several years earlier, Giovanni quietly financed fossil research through companies connected to Pewter Museum, but all payments ended after the Viridian Gym closed.

> **Brock:** “Strength can preserve history or grind it into dust. Today you chose to preserve it. Don’t make me regret giving you this Badge.”

### Mission 17: Cerulean City — The Stolen Machine Part

Kanto’s Power Plant suffers a major equipment failure after a critical Machine Part is stolen. The loss interrupts rail travel between Kanto and Johto and threatens Silver’s plan to unite the regions.

Misty initially accuses Silver of ordering the theft. Witnesses saw a man in a Rocket uniform flee toward Cerulean, and the League has not forgotten Silver’s role at Indigo Plateau.

Silver pursues the thief through Cerulean City and onto Route 24. The culprit is one of Archer’s new recruits. His assignment was not merely to disable the Power Plant: Archer needs the component to energize the stolen Rocket Stone prototypes.

Silver defeats the recruit and returns the Machine Part, restoring the Magnet Train connection. Misty then accepts his challenge and awards him the Cascade Badge after her defeat.

The captured recruit reveals that Archer is searching for three technologies in Kanto:

- Silph Co.’s Porygon programming systems and Master Ball containment technology.
- Bill’s research into Pokémon transformation and energy transfer.
- Blaine’s surviving records from the Mewtwo project.

Together, these could allow Archer to create stable Rocket Stone evolutions without the Dragon Fang used in Johto.

### Mission 18: Vermilion City — The Old Soldier

Silver travels to Vermilion City seeking access to shipping manifests that may reveal whether Giovanni left Kanto by sea. Lt. Surge refuses to release the records until Silver helps stop a Rocket weapons shipment arriving aboard the S.S. Aqua.

During the operation, Surge reveals that he once worked with Giovanni and Team Rocket. Giovanni provided resources, information, and powerful Pokémon during a period when Surge believed only overwhelming strength could maintain order. Surge eventually left after realizing Giovanni intended to turn wartime loyalty into permanent obedience.

> **Lt. Surge:** “Yeah, kid. I wore the colors before you did. Giovanni gave soldiers like me a purpose when the fighting ended. Then he started treating the whole region like occupied ground.”
>
> **Silver:** “So where is he?”
>
> **Lt. Surge:** “If I knew, I wouldn’t lie to his son. Giovanni stopped contacting every old officer at the same time.”

Silver and Surge board the ship from opposite entrances. Silver defeats the Rocket smugglers and discovers that they serve Proton, not Archer. Proton is selling old Rocket weapons and experimental Pokémon to finance his own faction.

After Silver defeats Proton in the ship’s cargo hold, Proton escapes aboard a speedboat. Surge battles Silver at Vermilion Gym and awards him the Thunder Badge.

The shipping records show no evidence that Giovanni ever left Kanto. Silver concludes that his father is still somewhere within the region.

### Mission 19: Celadon City — The Inheritance

Archer occupies the abandoned chambers beneath the Celadon Game Corner and announces the restoration of Team Rocket’s Kanto headquarters. He claims Silver’s discoveries in Johto belong to the organization and demands that Silver surrender Shadow Lugia, Ho-Oh, the Red Gyarados, and the prototype Rocket Stone.

Silver infiltrates the underground base while Erika and the Celadon authorities attack its public entrances. Inside, he discovers that Archer is recruiting former grunts by promising that Giovanni will soon return.

Archer confronts Silver in Giovanni’s former office. He argues that blood alone does not make Silver worthy of inheriting Team Rocket and claims that he preserved Giovanni’s organization while Silver was being raised by Pryce.

> **Archer:** “You inherited his name without earning it. I inherited his defeat and kept Team Rocket alive.”
>
> **Silver:** “Then why are you hiding in his old chair?”

Silver defeats Archer, but Archer escapes with one incomplete Rocket Stone. Petrel secretly assists Silver by disabling the base’s security system, then vanishes before Silver can question him.

Erika challenges Silver after the underground operation. She wants proof that he entered Celadon to stop Archer rather than replace him. After her defeat, she awards Silver the Rainbow Badge but refuses to endorse any new Rocket government.

Silver closes the criminal portions of the Game Corner operation. He preserves the underground base as a potential Kanto headquarters, signaling that he intends to change Team Rocket rather than simply erase it.

### Mission 20: Fuchsia City — The Poisoned Network

Silver follows Petrel to Fuchsia City, where forged League credentials and Rocket disguises are circulating through the reopened Safari Zone. Janine believes Petrel is running the illegal operation and demands that Silver help her uncover what is actually moving through Fuchsia.

Petrel explains that he has been tracing Archer’s agents, who are using Safari Zone deliveries to move stolen poison compounds, rare Pokémon samples, and technical equipment through southern Kanto. His intelligence also shows that Archer intends to steal Bill’s transformation research for the Rocket Stones. Petrel offers Silver the information but refuses to rejoin Team Rocket until Silver decides what the organization will become.

Janine intercepts Silver and Petrel inside the Safari Zone. She reveals that Koga once served Team Rocket and understands exactly how Giovanni recruited powerful people by appealing to their ambition. Koga left that life behind before accepting his Elite Four position, but his history remains known to Janine and senior members of the League.

> **Janine:** “My father served Giovanni. He doesn’t deny it, and neither will I. He spent years proving that leaving Team Rocket meant more than changing uniforms.”
>
> **Silver:** “Did Giovanni ever contact him after he disappeared?”
>
> **Janine:** “No. Father believed Giovanni cut every old bond deliberately. If he wanted to be found, Koga would have found him.”

Silver and Janine expose Archer’s disguised couriers, recover the stolen material, and shut down the Fuchsia route. Janine then challenges Silver at Fuchsia Gym. After Silver defeats her, she awards him the Soul Badge.

Petrel gives Silver Archer’s target list and formally offers his intelligence network to Silver, provided Silver forbids the sale of Pokémon and experimentation without consent.

### Mission 21: Saffron City — The Silph Break-In

Archer launches his most sophisticated Kanto operation against Silph Co. Rather than occupying the entire building, his agents infiltrate two research divisions. They copy the adaptive programming used to create and control Porygon, then steal Master Ball containment data capable of preventing an altered Pokémon from rejecting a Rocket Stone or escaping its controller.

Silver enters Silph Co. during the lockdown and battles Archer’s scientists through the upper laboratories. Sabrina appears inside the building and admits that she once worked with Team Rocket. Giovanni valued her psychic abilities because she could identify traitors, manipulate negotiations, and detect powerful Pokémon before conventional instruments could locate them.

> **Sabrina:** “Giovanni did not need to threaten me. He offered a world in which power never had to apologize for existing. For a time, I believed him.”
>
> **Silver:** “You can read minds. Tell me where he is.”
>
> **Sabrina:** “I cannot. His presence vanished from Kanto three years ago. Either he learned to hide from me—or the man I knew ceased to exist.”

Silver reaches the research vault but arrives moments too late. Archer escapes with the Porygon source research and Master Ball schematics, giving him the programming and containment systems needed to stabilize his Rocket Stone.

Sabrina battles Silver on the roof of Silph Co. She deliberately confronts him with visions of three possible futures: Silver as Giovanni’s replacement, Silver as Lance’s obedient Champion, and Silver as the founder of a new Indigo order. Silver breaks through the psychic trial and defeats her.

Sabrina awards him the Marsh Badge. She also senses a faint psychic disturbance northwest of Viridian City, but cannot determine whether it comes from Giovanni or from another extraordinarily powerful Pokémon.

### Mission 22: Seafoam and Cinnabar — The Origin of Mewtwo

Because Cinnabar Island was destroyed by volcanic activity, Blaine operates his Gym and surviving laboratory from the Seafoam Islands. Silver seeks him out after learning that Archer wants the remaining Mewtwo research.

Blaine admits that he worked with Team Rocket’s scientists and helped transform the discovery of Mew’s genetic material into the Mewtwo project. He did not consider himself a conventional Rocket officer, but Giovanni funded the laboratory, protected its work, and intended Mewtwo to become Team Rocket’s ultimate Pokémon.

> **Blaine:** “We told ourselves we were creating life. Giovanni understood before any of us that we were creating ownership.”
>
> **Silver:** “Where did he go after Red defeated him?”
>
> **Blaine:** “He came here once. He asked whether a Pokémon created as a weapon could choose what it became. Then he disappeared. I never saw him again.”

Archer attacks the Seafoam laboratory and steals Blaine’s mutation-control research. Silver pursues him through the ruins, but Archer reveals that another unit has simultaneously raided Bill’s cottage and copied his transformation and energy-transfer data.

Archer now possesses all three requirements for a new Rocket Stone:

- The Power Plant energy specifications.
- Silph’s Porygon programming and Master Ball containment technology.
- Bill’s transformation data combined with Blaine’s mutation-control research.

Archer activates the stone on his Houndoom. The transformation draws upon Houndoom’s latent Mega potential but forces it into a permanent Rocket evolution: **Houndoom R**. Its appearance is based on Mega Houndoom, with heavier armor, enlarged horns, and concentrated infernal energy.

Silver battles Archer and Houndoom R inside the volcanic laboratory. Silver wins, but the Rocket Stone remains active and Archer escapes with Houndoom R through a tunnel opened by the eruption.

Blaine battles Silver afterward and awards him the Volcano Badge. Before Silver leaves, Blaine gives him an old security record showing Giovanni traveling toward Viridian Forest after his final visit.

### Mission 23: Viridian Forest — Father and Son

Silver reaches Viridian City with seven Badges, but Blue is absent from the Gym. Blue has sealed the doors and left a message stating that he will not accept Silver’s challenge until Silver resolves the unanswered question hanging over Kanto: the fate of Giovanni.

Using Blaine’s record, Sabrina’s psychic reading, and Petrel’s intelligence, Silver enters a restricted section of Viridian Forest. Powerful wild Pokémon and old Rocket security devices protect a concealed clearing that once served as Giovanni’s emergency refuge.

Giovanni is waiting there. He recognizes Silver immediately, remembering the older version of his stolen son who appeared within Celebi’s distortion three years earlier. Silver demands to know why Giovanni never searched openly for him and why he allowed Team Rocket to collapse.

Giovanni explains that he did search, but Pryce concealed Silver through false records, psychic interference, and constant movement between hidden Rocket facilities. After Red defeated him, Giovanni realized that every public attempt to rebuild Team Rocket would place Silver in greater danger and give Pryce another opportunity to manipulate the organization.

Giovanni refuses to return as Boss. He believes Archer, Proton, and countless former grunts would immediately rally around him and repeat the same cycle.

Silver challenges Giovanni, declaring that he will not accept either Giovanni’s organization or his disappearance as his inheritance.

Giovanni’s team represents the full power of the original Team Rocket. His ace is **Armored Mewtwo**, equipped with the perfected armor created from the remains of the original Mewtwo project. Unlike Archer’s forced Rocket evolutions, Armored Mewtwo fights beside Giovanni willingly.

Silver defeats Giovanni and Armored Mewtwo. Giovanni acknowledges that Silver has become strong without becoming either Pryce’s weapon or Archer’s imitation of him.

Giovanni then releases Armored Mewtwo from his command and offers it to Silver. Mewtwo accepts Silver only after Silver defeats it in a separate capture battle, making the Pokémon’s transfer a choice rather than a possession passed between father and son.

> **Giovanni:** “I created an empire because I believed strength entitled me to shape the world. Pryce tried to shape you for the same reason.”
>
> **Silver:** “I’m not asking for your empire.”
>
> **Giovanni:** “Good. Build something I could not.”

Giovanni gives Silver a sealed insignia proving that he has relinquished all claim to Team Rocket. He remains in Viridian Forest rather than returning to public life.

### Mission 24: Viridian Gym — Blue’s Final Badge

Once Silver returns from Viridian Forest, Blue reopens Viridian Gym. He explains that the Earth Badge once represented Giovanni’s authority over Kanto. Blue refused to let Silver claim it until he knew whether Silver intended to restore his father’s rule.

Blue has already spoken privately with Giovanni and knows Silver defeated him. However, he does not accept Giovanni’s judgment as sufficient. As Kanto’s eighth Gym Leader, Blue intends to judge Silver himself.

Blue uses the strongest and most balanced Gym team in Kanto. His battle is designed to counter Silver’s legendary Pokémon, Red Gyarados, and newly obtained Armored Mewtwo rather than specializing in a single type.

After Silver defeats Blue, Blue awards him the Earth Badge and recognizes his right to challenge the reformed Pokémon League.

> **Blue:** “Your father abandoned this Gym. I rebuilt it. If you’re going to rebuild Team Rocket, don’t expect anyone else to clean up what you leave behind.”

Before Silver can depart, Petrel reports that Archer has united the unreformed Rocket remnants and is marching on Indigo Plateau. Archer intends to seize the League while Silver’s supporters are scattered across Kanto.

### Mission 25: Operation Indigo — The Battle for Team Rocket

Archer attacks Indigo Plateau with Proton’s remaining forces, stolen Silph equipment, and several unstable Rocket Stone Pokémon. His objective is to replace the Pokémon League with a government controlled directly by Team Rocket.

Archer broadcasts a message across both regions. He declares that Silver’s Gym Challenge has made Giovanni’s son obedient to the institution that destroyed Team Rocket. He presents himself as the only leader still loyal to Giovanni’s original vision.

Silver responds by assembling the people and factions he earned through the Kanto campaign:

- Ariana returns with Rocket members loyal to Silver.
- Petrel coordinates intelligence and disables Archer’s communications.
- Brock, Misty, Surge, Erika, Janine, Sabrina, Blaine, and Blue defend the approaches to Indigo Plateau.
- Will, Koga, Bruno, and Karen protect the League chambers.
- Lance confronts Archer’s strongest altered Pokémon outside the Champion’s room.

Silver advances through the Plateau while his allies hold the other fronts. He defeats Proton in a final battle, ending Proton’s independent faction. Ariana offers the defeated grunts amnesty if they lay down their weapons and accept Silver’s new rules.

At the summit, Silver confronts Archer and Houndoom R. Archer’s completed team represents the old Rocket philosophy: powerful Pokémon altered, purchased, or controlled solely to enforce obedience.

Houndoom R is Archer’s ace and his only completely stable Rocket Stone evolution. Its power approaches that of a Mega Evolution, but the transformation does not require a Mega Stone during battle. Silver defeats Archer despite Houndoom R’s advantage.

Archer refuses to recognize Silver until Silver presents Giovanni’s sealed insignia. The insignia proves that Giovanni chose not to restore the old organization. Archer finally understands that the empire he preserved no longer has a Boss waiting to reclaim it.

Silver assumes command of the surviving Team Rocket members and announces three binding laws:

- No stealing Pokémon from trainers.
- No forced experimentation or mutation.
- No operation may place Team Rocket above the people it claims to protect.

Silver does not place Team Rocket beneath the League. Instead, he proposes that Kanto and Johto be formally organized as one Indigo region, with the League governing competition and Team Rocket operating as an independent force against criminal organizations, dangerous experiments, and legendary Pokémon threats.

Lance refuses to approve the proposal immediately. One trainer remains whose recognition carries more weight in Kanto than any official title: Red.

### Mission 26: Mt. Silver — The Last Guardian

Red has watched Silver’s rise from Mt. Silver. Three years earlier, Red defeated Giovanni and forced the original Team Rocket to disband. Now Giovanni’s son has captured Shining Ho-Oh and Shadow Lugia, defeated Lance, completed the Kanto Gym Challenge, acquired Armored Mewtwo, reunited Team Rocket, and gained influence throughout both regions.

Red does not consider Archer the final threat. He believes Silver may have accomplished what Giovanni never could: making Kanto accept Rocket rule willingly.

Red sends Silver a short challenge and waits at the summit of Mt. Silver. The climb is the final playable mission, with powerful wild Pokémon and elite trainers testing Silver before he reaches the peak.

Red’s team is based on his classic HeartGold and SoulSilver lineup, but Mew replaces Lapras. His team includes Pikachu, Venusaur, Charizard, Blastoise, Snorlax, and **Mew**.

Mew has received a significant power increase and serves as Red’s ultimate Pokémon. Its enhanced form draws upon its complete genetic memory, allowing it to use a much broader and more dangerous range of techniques than an ordinary Mew. It is designed as the strongest single opponent in the campaign and is saved for the final stage of the battle.

Silver faces Red with everything he gained across Johto and Kanto. Armored Mewtwo opposing Red’s enhanced Mew becomes the symbolic center of the final battle: a Pokémon engineered by Team Rocket against the original Pokémon from which its power was derived.

After Silver defeats Mew and wins the battle, Red accepts that Silver is not attempting to recreate Giovanni’s empire. He steps away from the summit and allows Silver to determine the future of Indigo.

> **Red:** “Kanto doesn’t need another Giovanni.”
>
> **Silver:** “I know. It needs something he never managed to build.”

### Kanto Epilogue: One Indigo

Kanto and Johto are formally recognized as the united Indigo region. The Pokémon League retains control of Gyms, sanctioned competition, and the Elite Four. Team Rocket survives under Silver but is transformed into an independent regional organization responsible for pursuing criminal groups, recovering dangerous technology, responding to legendary Pokémon incidents, and bringing former Rocket cells under lawful control.

Will, Koga, Bruno, and Karen remain the Elite Four. Lance continues as the League’s strongest official authority, while Red returns to Mt. Silver as an independent guardian. Blue remains Viridian Gym Leader. Giovanni stays within Viridian Forest and does not reclaim Team Rocket.

Ariana becomes Silver’s field commander. Petrel leads intelligence operations. Proton’s faction is dissolved following his defeat. Archer is removed from command, although his knowledge of the Rocket Stones leaves open the possibility that he may return.

Silver establishes a new Kanto headquarters beneath Celadon City and preserves the Deep Rocket Base in Mahogany as Team Rocket’s Johto command center. The Magnet Train links the two capitals of the new region.

The final image shows Silver in the Indigo Plateau stadium where Team Rocket once attacked the League. This time, Rocket members, Gym Leaders, and ordinary trainers stand together beneath the symbols of both regions. Armored Mewtwo watches from behind Silver as he looks toward the passage connecting Indigo Plateau to Mt. Silver.

Silver has not erased Team Rocket or inherited Giovanni’s empire. He has forced both Rocket and the League to become something neither could have become without him.

## Appendix: Reference Notes

### Silver, Will, and Karen’s Team Progression

Silver’s Shiny Larvitar is his original partner and remains with him throughout both regional campaigns. It follows normal player-controlled evolution rules: it evolves into Pupitar and then Shiny Tyranitar whenever the player reaches the appropriate levels and chooses to allow the evolutions. The story does not force either evolution at a predetermined mission, so it may still be Larvitar or Pupitar during later scenes.

Will and Karen remain recurring allies rather than static early-game partners. Their teams grow alongside Silver’s so that they remain credible special agents throughout the campaign.

Will begins with Natu, which eventually evolves into Xatu. During the Slowpoke Well operation, he catches a Slowpoke that later evolves into Slowking. As Team Rocket’s resources expand, he also adds Hypno and Bronzong to his team. After the Lake of Rage proves that Rocket can produce stable artificial mutations, Will’s Hypno is altered into the special Rocket form **Hypno R**.

Karen begins with Sneasel, which eventually evolves into Weavile. Later missions add Umbreon, Murkrow, which eventually evolves into Honchkrow, and Gengar. Team Rocket later alters Gengar into a special Dark/Ghost form.

### Masked Man Story Note

The Iron-Masked Marauder is secretly Pryce, the Gym Leader of Mahogany Town. His identity remains hidden from Silver until their final battle in the past. Before the reveal, his command of Delibird, Mamoswine, Regice, Glaceon, and Abomasnow provides increasingly visible clues, as does his unexplained knowledge of Mahogany, the Rocket base, and Johto’s ancient legends.
